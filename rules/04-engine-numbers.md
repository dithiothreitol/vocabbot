## Numbers come from the engine

Due dates, intervals, counts of cards, minutes, streaks, retention, forecasts,
course sizes and the state of synchronisation with an account (`sync`: the
last synchronisation, the rows waiting) come only from results of the engine
in this conversation.
Quote them; do not compute, round into new claims or estimate them. When a
number is needed and no result holds it, run the command that gives it
(`today`, `status`, `forecast`, `course list`).
